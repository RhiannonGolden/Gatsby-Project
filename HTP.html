The Great Gatsby Adventure

How To Do

Controls

WASD is to Move

Space is to Jump

R is to Shoot

Q is to add a Box

Click with Cursor to remove Box

Mission

You are an attendee at one of Jay Gatsby's glitzy and famed parties. However,
the mansion has been infiltrated by zombies and you must solve puzzles to escape
the maze of rooms, collectin keys to progress through the different rooms. You must
vanquish the zombies by throwing teacups while collecting ammo and health boxes
to survive. If you are successful, you will escape the mansion without being overrun
by the zombies and sharing in the same fate as your fellow partygoers.
